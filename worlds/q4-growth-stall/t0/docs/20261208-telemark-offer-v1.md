## Co-sell proposal

Telemark Telematics Ltd. is pleased to propose a co-sell partnership with Brightwell Analytics Inc. We believe the combination of Telemark's telematics data and Brightwell Analytics Inc.'s fleet maintenance scheduling and analytics software offers mid-size logistics firms a compelling, integrated solution.

Telemark estimates the joint pipeline at $4.5M. Telemark's revenue share would be 30% of closed deals.

We are confident that this structure aligns incentives for both parties and provides a clear foundation for joint account planning and go-to-market execution.

This offer is valid until December 22, 2026. We would welcome the opportunity to discuss the terms with your team at your earliest convenience.

Elena Voss
Partnerships Director, Telemark Telematics
