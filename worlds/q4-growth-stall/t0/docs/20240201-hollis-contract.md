## Fees

In consideration of access to the Brightwell Analytics Inc. fleet maintenance scheduling and analytics platform, Hollis Freight LLC shall pay Brightwell Analytics Inc. an annual subscription fee of $3.2M. The fee shall be invoiced in advance and is payable in accordance with the payment terms agreed between the parties.

## Term

This agreement shall remain in force from its effective date and shall expire on January 31, 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its provisions or extended by written agreement signed by both parties.
