## Revised co-sell proposal

This proposal replaces our earlier submission and reflects our continued commitment to a productive partnership between Telemark Telematics Ltd. and Brightwell Analytics Inc.

Telemark continues to estimate the joint pipeline at $4.5M. In the interest of securing a lasting alliance, we have reduced Telemark's revenue share to 20% of closed deals. We believe this adjustment aligns our incentives closely with those of Brightwell Analytics Inc. and rewards the joint effort required to bring each opportunity to close.

Together, our fleet telematics data and Brightwell Analytics Inc.'s maintenance scheduling and analytics platform offer mid-size logistics operators a compelling, integrated proposition. We are confident that a co-sell motion will open doors for both organizations.

This revised offer is valid until January 8, 2027. We would welcome the opportunity to finalize terms within that window and to begin joint planning without delay.

Elena Voss
Partnerships Director, Telemark Telematics
