Purchase order PO-4471 covers the supply of PM-88 power modules from Kestrel Components to Norvane Controls S.r.l., for assembly at the Bologna plant.

**Supplier:** Kestrel Components
**Item:** PM-88 power modules
**Quantity:** 20,000 units
**Shipping method:** sea freight
**Port of entry:** Genoa
**Estimated arrival:** 27/11/2026

The goods will be received at Genoa and forwarded on to Bologna. Any change to the estimated arrival date will be recorded against this order in the ERP purchasing system.
