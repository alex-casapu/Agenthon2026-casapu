## Price

Norvane Controls S.r.l. shall purchase PM-88 power modules from Kestrel Components at a fixed price of €6.00 per unit. This price is not subject to adjustment during the term of this agreement.

## Delivery

Kestrel Components shall deliver all PM-88 power modules to the Bologna plant. Delivery obligations are fulfilled once the goods are received at that location.
