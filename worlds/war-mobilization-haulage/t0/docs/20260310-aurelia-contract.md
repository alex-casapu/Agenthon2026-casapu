## Minimum Capacity

Kestrel Haulage Ltd shall keep at the disposal of Aurelia Retail S.A. a minimum fleet of 80 trucks, each with its driver, on every working day for the delivery of retail goods.

## Penalty

For each truck missing below the minimum capacity on any working day, Kestrel Haulage Ltd shall pay Aurelia Retail S.A. a penalty of 400 € per truck per day.

## Term

This agreement shall remain in force until it expires on 30/06/2027.
