## Fleet and staffing

Kestrel Haulage Ltd currently employs 120 drivers and operates 96 trucks. Both figures cover our own fleet and our own drivers only, with no subcontracted capacity included.

Driver rosters and truck allocations for the month are set against the delivery schedules we have agreed with our retail customers. Operations will keep adjusting daily allocations as needed to keep trucks on the road and deliveries on time across Orsenia.
